## Shot details

### Shot 1 — Hook · f0–f47 (00:00:00:00 → 00:00:01:23)
- **Frame:** the gateway stands centred in a dark architectural space; base at 73 % of frame height, top at 28 %.
  The lower 20 % (overlay zone) stays calm: floor and haze only.
- **Action beats:** f1 pulse appears at frame left, low; f1–f44 it accelerates along a curved floor path toward the
  threshold with a short fading trail; f34–f47 a faint red spill on the threshold; f44 contact; f44–f47 the pulse
  flares small and the camera keeps pushing.
- **What "exposed" means visually:** one section of the gate's teal edge is dim and flickering — a gap in an
  otherwise continuous light line. No broken glass, sparks or fire.
- **Overlay:** "One weak point is enough." fades/rises in over f0–f5, holds, fades out f42–f47.

### Shot 2 — Product · f48–f119 (00:00:02:00 → 00:00:04:23)
- **Frame:** network centred at 42 % of frame height; nothing important below 70 % (overlay zone).
- **Action beats:** f48–f61 the camera passes through the opening (the gate frame rushes past the lens and
  clears); f52–f67 the platform and hub fade up; f59 **website** node (browser-window glyph), f68 **email** node
  (envelope), f78 **account** node (key) — each with a soft flash and a light line drawn to the hub; f86–f101 faint
  cross-links between nodes; f79 a tiny red trace appears at the far edge and drifts toward the network.
- **Overlay:** "Cybersecurity for businesses and individuals." f54–f119 (enters 6 frames after the cut so the
  pass-through reads first).

### Shot 3 — Benefit · f120–f191 (00:00:05:00 → 00:00:07:23)
- **Frame:** same network position; the slight pull-back makes room for the three rings.
- **Action beats:** layer 1 forms f121 → locks f133; layer 2 forms f130 → locks f142; layer 3 forms f138 →
  locks f150 (each lock is a brief brightening of the ring edge). The red trace keeps travelling and meets the
  outer layer at **f158**: it stops, turns ash-grey (#6B7688) and disperses upward; a teal ripple runs around the
  outer ring. Nodes shift from soft white to teal f164–f182 — the network is now protected *in the symbolic
  sense of the scene* (the copy never promises total protection).
- **Overlay:** "Understand your risks." enters f126; "Strengthen your defenses." enters f138; both fade out
  f186–f191.
- **Transition:** fade to black f188–f191.

### Shot 4 — Brand · f192–f239 (00:00:08:00 → 00:00:09:23)
- **Frame:** gateway in the lower half (top ≈ 50 %, base ≈ 89 % of frame height); the upper 45 % is dark and
  empty — the real logo goes there in editing.
- **Action beats:** f192–f202 up from black; f193–f212 a teal light traces the full chamfered frame; f199–f218
  the floor grid fades in faintly; f206–f225 the vertical door seam glows; f235–f239 seam brightens before opening.
- **Overlay:** stacked logo (mark + wordmark) from `assets/overlays/<fmt>/ov4-brand.svg`; the mark's two strokes
  draw on f198–f215, the wordmark fades/rises in f204–f215; cross-dissolve into the end-card lockup f240–f245.

### Shot 5 — CTA · f240–f287 (00:00:10:00 → 00:00:11:23)
- **Frame:** same as shot 4 at the start; the dolly forward (≈10 % in 16:9, ≈8 % in 9:16) keeps the upper 45 %
  clear for the end card.
- **Action beats:** f238–f250 a teal glow appears in the seam; f240–f271 the panels slide apart smoothly
  (ease-in-out); f245–f287 the teal horizon and floor light rise; the last frame holds the open gate.
- **Overlay:** horizontal lockup (`ovL-endcard-lockup`) and "Start with a security assessment." fade/rise in over
  f240–f245; slogan "Your gateway to a safer digital world." fades in f246–f251; all hold to f287.
