## Delivery

- **Voice:** calm, warm authority; low-to-mid register; clear consonants. Vigilant, precise, approachable — never a
  trailer voice, never a whisper, no "hacker" processing (no distortion, no robot filter).
- **Hook:** measured and quiet, not alarmed. **Product / Benefit:** matter-of-fact, helpful. **Brand:** simple,
  unhurried. **CTA:** open and inviting, a slight smile.
- **Recording:** close mic in a treated room, dry (no reverb), 48 kHz / 24-bit. Record 3 takes per line plus a full
  run-through; keep 0.5 s handles.
- **Processing:** high-pass ≈ 80 Hz, gentle de-ess, light compression (2–3 dB gain reduction), peaks ≤ −3 dBFS
  before the master. Duck the sound bed 3–4 dB under each line ([05](05-sound-design.md)).

## Copy rules (do not change these when adapting the script)

- No guarantees or absolutes: never "you're secure", "completely safe", "fully protected", "100 %",
  "guaranteed", "unhackable", "military-grade". The script uses *help*, *understand*, *strengthen*, *start*; the
  slogan's comparative "safer" is the strongest word allowed.
- No fictional incidents, statistics, customer names or certifications.
- The voice never says the threat is real; the picture shows a symbolic, simulated pulse.

## Talent and rights

Use a voice actor who has agreed to advertising use (written release covering platforms and duration). If a
synthetic voice is used instead, check that its licence allows advertising, and never clone or imitate a real
person's voice without their consent.
